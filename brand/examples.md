# Gold-standard example posts

These five posts define what "sounds like Servio" means. The AI writer reads
them before generating anything, and new drafts should feel like they belong in
this set: practical, plain English, real numbers only, one idea, exactly one
call to action, no hype.

Notes on the examples:

- Links are shown clean, without tracking. The pipeline appends the UTM
  tracking parameters automatically at publish time.
- Instagram captions say "link in bio" because links in Instagram captions are
  not clickable.
- No example quotes a statistic, because none of these needed one. When a real,
  verifiable number exists it is welcome; an invented one is never acceptable.

---

## Example 1 — LinkedIn (education, CTA: site-audit)

> A slow website loses customers you never even meet.
>
> Here is a 60-second check you can do right now:
>
> 1. Open your site on your phone, on mobile data, not Wi-Fi.
> 2. Count the seconds before you can read the headline.
> 3. Try to tap the contact button with your thumb.
>
> If any of those made you wait or squint, your visitors felt it too. Most
> will not wait. They tap back and try the next result.
>
> Speed is not a technical detail. It is the first impression of your
> business.
>
> Want to know exactly what is holding your site back? Ask us for a site
> audit: https://servio-0.web.app/contact

**Why it is on-brand:** it teaches something the reader can do in 60 seconds, uses countable numbers instead of invented statistics, and ends on exactly one CTA.

## Example 2 — LinkedIn (service, CTA: get-quote)

> Most small businesses do not need a big website. They need one page that
> does one job.
>
> A landing page has one job: turn a visitor into an enquiry. That means:
>
> - One offer, stated in the first line.
> - One short form a visitor can finish in under a minute.
> - One button, visible without scrolling.
>
> Everything else is decoration.
>
> We build landing pages starting at ₹7,999. Ready to talk about yours? Get a
> quote here: https://servio-0.web.app/contact

**Why it is on-brand:** it sells by simplifying rather than hyping, and the only price it quotes is a real one taken straight from brand.yml.

## Example 3 — X (education, CTA: site-audit)

> Your website has one job: make it easy to say yes.
>
> Quick test: can a stranger find your phone number on your homepage in 10
> seconds? If not, you are losing enquiries.
>
> Site audit: https://servio-0.web.app/contact

**Why it is on-brand:** one sharp idea sized for X, with a test the reader can run immediately, and it is a fresh variant rather than a copy of the LinkedIn post.

## Example 4 — Instagram caption (education, CTA: site-audit)

> Three questions to ask before you redesign your website:
>
> 1. What is the one action you want a visitor to take?
> 2. Can they do it on a phone in under a minute?
> 3. What is stopping them today?
>
> If you cannot answer number 3, that is where we come in. We find what is
> blocking your visitors, then fix it.
>
> Tap the link in our bio to request a site audit.
>
> #SmallBusinessIndia #WebsiteDesign #WebDevelopment

**Why it is on-brand:** platform-appropriate (link in bio, three targeted hashtags, no emoji wall) while staying a practical checklist with a single CTA.

## Example 5 — Facebook (pain-point, CTA: get-quote)

> Still running your business on WhatsApp and Instagram alone?
>
> That works, until a customer wants to check your prices at 11 pm. Or find
> your address. Or see whether you look trustworthy before ordering. A simple
> website answers those questions while you sleep.
>
> It does not need to be big. Five pages cover most small businesses: home,
> services, pricing, about, contact.
>
> Thinking about getting online? Send us a message or get a quote here:
> https://servio-0.web.app/contact

**Why it is on-brand:** warm and conversational for Facebook, speaks to a real pain point from brand.yml, and never resorts to engagement bait or invented numbers.
