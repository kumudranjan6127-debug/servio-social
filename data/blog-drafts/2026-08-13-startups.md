# How to Stop Wasting Hours Answering Repetitive Messages When You Launch

Opening a new business is exciting. You spend weeks getting your products ready, decorating your space, and telling friends and family. But as soon as your doors open, a new daily task takes over your schedule: replying to constant WhatsApp messages.

"Where are you located?"
"What are your opening hours?"
"How much does this item cost?"
"Do you have parking space available?"

When you answer these messages manually one by one, you lose hours of focus every single day. Even worse, if you take too long to reply while busy with an in-store customer, the online buyer might move on to a competitor.

Here is how you can organize your business details so customers get immediate answers without you typing the same text fifty times.

## 1. Set Up Your WhatsApp Business Catalog
If you use a standard phone account for business, switch to the free WhatsApp Business app. It allows you to create a digital product catalog with clear pricing and photos right inside the app. When customers ask about prices, you can share a direct catalog link in two taps instead of looking up photos and typing prices every time.

## 2. Claim Your Google Business Profile
Many people ask for your address simply because they cannot find you on Google Maps. Take ten minutes to claim and fill out your Google Business Profile. Add your exact store location, working hours, photos of your storefront, and parking details. When anyone searches for your business name on Google, they will get full directions instantly.

## 3. Use Quick Replies for Frequent Questions
Inside WhatsApp Business, you can save pre-written messages called Quick Replies. If people frequently ask about payment options or store policies, you can store a friendly response and send it in seconds by typing a simple shortcut.

## 4. Build a Simple Single-Page Website
Social media posts quickly get buried. A single-page website acts as a permanent home for your business details. Place a clear FAQ section right near the top of the page covering your address, prices, order process, and contact details.

Put this website link inside your social media bios and set it as your WhatsApp away message. When new visitors click your link, they find everything they need in one clean spot.

## Get Your Time Back
You started your business to create products, cook great food, or help your local clients—not to sit on your phone typing your address all day. Giving customers direct access to basic information builds trust and frees you up to grow your business.
