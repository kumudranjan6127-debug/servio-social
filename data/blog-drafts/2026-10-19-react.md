# What Small Business Owners Should Know About React Before Hiring an Agency

When you decide to build a new website for your business, agencies often throw technical terms your way. One word you will almost certainly hear is React.

It is often pitched as modern, fast, and essential. But as a busy business owner, you need to know what it actually means for your wallet, your time, and your customer enquiries.

Here are five straightforward things you should know before signing a proposal.

### 1. It Might Be Expensive Overkill
React was built to power dynamic, highly interactive tools—think social platforms, live dashboards, or complex booking software. If your main goal is simple—introducing your dental clinic, showing your bakery's menu, or sharing your salon hours—paying for a custom React build is usually unnecessary. A clean, standard business website will do that job faster and for a lower cost.

### 2. You Will Not Be Locked In
One big danger with custom websites is vendor lock-in. A developer builds your site on an obscure platform, disappears, and leaves you unable to find anyone else who can update it. React is the most popular tool of its kind in the world. If your current agency stops responding, another agency or freelancer can easily pick up where they left off.

### 3. Google Visibility Needs Extra Care
By default, standard React setups build pages inside the visitor's browser. This can sometimes make it difficult for search engines to read your content and show your business on search result pages. If an agency uses React for your site, ask them directly how they ensure Google can read every page properly so you do not lose local customers.

### 4. Plan for Ongoing Upkeep
React sites rely on an ecosystem of smaller code packages. Over time, these pieces need routine updates to stay secure and run smoothly. If you choose this path, make sure you budget for regular maintenance so your site does not become vulnerable down the road.

### 5. A Head Start on Future Phone Apps
If you plan to launch a dedicated mobile app for your business later, choosing React now can be a smart investment. Developers can reuse much of the underlying structure for a mobile app, saving you both time and development costs.

### Choosing the Right Fit
You do not need to be a developer to make smart decisions for your website. The key is picking the right solution for your specific goals rather than paying for technical trends you do not need.
