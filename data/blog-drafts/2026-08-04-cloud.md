# How to Keep Your Business Photos, Price Lists, and Contacts Safe If Your Laptop Dies

Every small business owner relies heavily on their shop laptop. Whether you run a bakery, dental clinic, clothing boutique, or local service shop, that single computer holds your price lists, customer records, and product photos. 

But what happens if that laptop gets damaged, stolen, or simply stops turning on? If everything is stored locally on your hard drive, a single hardware failure can halt your daily operations and wipe out years of work.

Fortunately, keeping your business data completely safe is simple and affordable. By storing your information on the internet—often called the cloud—you ensure that your business stays open even if your computer breaks.

## 5 Simple Ways to Protect Your Business Files

### 1. Auto-Sync Your Photos and Media
Instead of saving product photos only in folders on your computer desktop, use tools like Google Drive or OneDrive. Set them to sync automatically with your media folders. Any picture you add on your computer will copy straight to the web, so you can access it from your mobile phone anytime.

### 2. Store Customer Contacts in Online Tools
Spreadsheets saved on a local desktop are easy to lose. Move customer names, phone numbers, and email addresses into an online address book or contact tool that you can open from any web browser on any device.

### 3. Maintain Live Online Price Lists
Keep master price sheets and inventory notes in live documents like Google Sheets. When you make an edit, it saves instantly on the web off your device. You will always have the latest prices ready to show customers, even from your phone.

### 4. Schedule Automatic Website Backups
Your website is your digital storefront. Ensure your web platform or host is set up to create automatic offsite backups. If something ever goes wrong, your site content, photos, and customer enquiry forms can be restored quickly.

### 5. Use an Online Password Manager
Forgetting or losing website logins when a laptop breaks can lock you out of your account for days. A secure online password manager keeps your accounts organized and accessible from any new device securely.

## Never Lose Business to a Hardware Failure

When your customer details, product images, and price lists live safely online, a broken laptop is just a brief inconvenience—not a business crisis. You can log in from another screen and keep serving customers without missing a beat.
