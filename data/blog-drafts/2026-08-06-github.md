# Never Lose Control of Your Website Again: The Safe Master Copy System

Every month, small business owners face a stressful nightmare. They hire a freelance web designer to build a site for their restaurant, clinic, or boutique. Everything goes well at first. But a few months later, the business owner wants to make a simple change—like updating prices or adding a new service.

They send a message to the designer. No reply. They call. No answer.

When they reach out to a new designer for help, they get bad news: "We do not have access to your main files. We will have to rebuild the entire website from scratch."

Just like that, thousands of rupees and hours of work are lost. You are forced to pay twice for the exact same website.

Here is how you can make sure this never happens to your business.

## Always Hold the Master Key

When a website is built, the original files need a safe home. Too often, designers keep these files on their personal laptops or inside private accounts you cannot see.

Instead, your primary website vault should always be created using your official business email address. When a designer works on your site, you invite them as a guest collaborator. 

You remain the main owner. You hold the master key.

## Total Security and Fast Handovers

Storing your master copy in a centralized, secure cloud vault brings three massive advantages for your peace of mind:

1. **Instant Access Revocation:** If a freelancer stops responding or you end your contract with an agency, you can remove their access in seconds. They cannot lock you out or alter your site.
2. **A Complete Change History:** The vault keeps a timestamped record of every single change made to your site. If someone makes a mistake or a page stops working, you can roll back to an earlier, perfect version.
3. **Smooth Transitions:** If you ever hire a new helper, they can look at the saved files and take over immediately. No rebuild fees, no wasted time, and no stress.
