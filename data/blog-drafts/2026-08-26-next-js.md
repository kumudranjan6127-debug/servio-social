# Common Modern Website Mistakes Small Businesses Make (And Simple Fixes)

When you hire someone to build a website for your clinic, salon, or store, you want one simple outcome: people visit, trust your business, and get in touch.

Today, many modern websites are built using high-performance foundations like Next.js. When used properly, this technology makes pages load in the blink of an eye. But when set up carelessly, it can lead to slow pages, frustrated visitors, and lost calls.

Here are the most common mistakes small businesses encounter, and how to fix them.

### 1. Uploading Massive, Heavy Photos

You take crisp photos of your store, team, or products with a new smartphone and put them straight onto the page. But a high-resolution photo file can be huge. On a regular mobile connection, downloading that single photo can freeze the screen for several seconds.

**The simple fix:** Modern web setups can automatically shrink images to fit the exact size of the visitor's screen. Make sure your site uses automatic image optimization so visitors get crisp visuals without wasting data or waiting around.

### 2. Hiding Important Text From Search Engines

Some websites load content in pieces only after a person opens the page. While this can work for private accounts, it is a problem for public business websites. When Google scans your site to understand what services you offer in your city, it might find empty space instead of your service list and address.

**The simple fix:** Ensure your main text, prices, and contact details are pre-rendered. That means the whole page is already prepared and ready to read before anyone even clicks the link.

### 3. Adding Heavy Effects That Nobody Needs

Fancy moving graphics and floating buttons might look clever, but they often slow down phones and distract visitors who just want your phone number, opening hours, or location.

**The simple fix:** Keep the design clean and clear. Prioritize fast answers to customer questions over complicated visual tricks.

### Build for the Customer First

A great business website does not need to be complicated. It just needs to open instantly, look professional, and make it effortless for customers to choose you.
