# Why Hidden Phone Numbers and Prices Cost You Customers (And How to Fix It)

When someone visits your website, they usually want an answer to two basic questions: "How do I contact you?" and "Roughly how much does this cost?"

If finding those answers feels like a treasure hunt, most visitors will not bother digging. They will simply close your site and move on to a competitor.

## The Multi-Tab Comparison Habit

Think about how you shop on your phone. When looking for a local dentist, salon, or caterer, you rarely open just one website. You open three or four in separate browser tabs and switch between them.

The business that clearly shows its phone number, location, and starting rates in seconds wins the first phone call. If your site hides details behind deep menus or forces visitors to fill out a contact form just to ask for a phone number, you lose that buyer before you even get a chance to talk to them.

## Hiding Rates Creates Bad Impressions

Many small business owners hide their prices because they want customers to call first. But online buyers often assume that hidden prices mean two things:

1. The service is too expensive for their budget.
2. They will be pushed into a high-pressure sales conversation.

Even broad starting prices give buyers the confidence they need to pick up the phone. Transparency builds trust before a conversation even begins.

## Simple Fixes That Drive Real Results

You do not need a complex online presence to win more customers. A few straightforward changes make an immediate difference:

- **One-tap phone calls:** Place a clear call button right at the top of your page on mobile screens.
- **Clear starting rates:** List starting prices for popular services so visitors know if you fit their budget.
- **Direct contact info:** Keep your address and main phone number clearly visible on every page.

When you remove the hassle, getting new inquiries becomes effortless.
