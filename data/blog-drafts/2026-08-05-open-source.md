# Why "Free" Website Software Costs Small Businesses More in the Long Run

When you are starting or growing a local business, saving money is always a top priority. Whether you run a bakery, a dental clinic, or a clothing boutique, picking "free" website software seems like an easy decision. 

However, many shop owners quickly discover that free tools often come with hidden costs that appear when you least expect them.

## 1. Updates That Clash and Cause Breakdown

Free website tools rely on dozens of small extra programs to add feature items like photo galleries, maps, or contact forms. Every week, these separate tools publish minor updates.

When one piece updates and another does not, they often clash. Suddenly, customers cannot view your menu or book an appointment. Because there is no central helpline to call, you must pay a private technician by the hour to trace the mistake and fix it.

## 2. No Helpline When Things Go Wrong

When your card payment machine breaks, you call the provider for support. But free software does not come with a dedicated customer service team.

If your website goes offline during a busy holiday sales period, you are entirely on your own. You have no choice but to pay emergency hourly rates to a web technician just to get your site back online.

## 3. High Risk of Automated Bot Attacks

Because the building blocks of free software are completely public, software bots constantly sweep the internet looking for unmanaged websites. 

If your site goes without frequent hands-on maintenance, these bots can place unwanted spam on your pages or take your site down entirely. Cleaning up a hacked site costs significantly more than maintaining a solid site from the start.

## 4. Custom Changes Break Over Time

If you pay someone to add a custom feature to a free platform—like a custom price calculator—it might work great today. But when the core software releases a major update next year, that custom work often stops working.

You end up paying the technician all over again to rebuild work you already paid for in the past.
